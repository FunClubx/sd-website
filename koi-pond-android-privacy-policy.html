Privacy Policy

Koi Pond - 3D Live Wallpaper

Android · Effective date: September 8, 2026 · Version 1.1

1. Who we are

Guangzhou Suide Trading Co., Ltd. ("Suide Trading", "we", "us", or "our") operates Koi Pond - 3D Live Wallpaper for Android (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including its live wallpaper, advertising, video-sharing, and feedback features.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@suidetrading.com.

Summary: The App does not require an account. Pond progress and preferences are primarily stored on your device and may be included in Android system backups. We use Firebase for analytics, crash diagnosis, and remote configuration, and Unity advertising services to support the App. You can manage optional analytics, diagnostics, and third-party advertising in Settings under Privacy Choices. Remote configuration and promotional-content downloads may still make network requests when these optional services are disabled. If you choose to submit feedback, we receive your message, selected reasons, and basic App and device information to help investigate the issue.

2. Information handled by the App

Local progress and system backups

The App stores pond progress, virtual coin and food balances, unlocked items, selected koi and themes, tutorial state, sound and visual settings, privacy choices, and reward timing on your device. These records support App and live wallpaper features. Virtual-item exchanges use the App's virtual currency; the current version does not collect payment-card details or provide in-app real-money purchases.

Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up or transfer App data and restore it when you reinstall the App or move to another device. We do not provide an App account or an account-based cloud save service. System backup and transfer are controlled through your device settings and the relevant provider's policies.

Usage and diagnostics

When optional analytics and diagnostics are enabled, we and our service providers may process:

  • App interactions, koi and theme selections, feeding actions, virtual-item exchanges, reward events, advertising events and revenue, video-feature interactions, session duration, and summarized live wallpaper usage;
  • App and operating-system versions, device model, language, country or approximate region, installation or app-instance identifiers, and technical configuration;
  • Crash reports, stack traces, native crash diagnostics, errors, and technical information used to understand and resolve failures.

Our App analytics events do not include your name, email address, messages, contacts, photos, video contents, or precise location. We do not request permission to access contacts or precise location. Service providers may derive an approximate region from network information such as an IP address.

Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config retrieves settings that control App features, rewards, and promotional content. These requests may involve an IP address, a Firebase installation identifier, App and device versions, language, country code, and time zone. Remote configuration operates separately from optional analytics and may continue when you decline or disable optional services.

Advertising

The App supports rewarded and interstitial ads through Unity LevelPlay, ironSource, and Unity Ads. Rewarded ads provide virtual rewards after an eligible ad is completed. Advertising providers may process IP addresses, approximate location, device and App information, Android advertising identifiers when available, installation identifiers, ad impressions, clicks and completions, attribution information, fraud-prevention signals, and diagnostics.

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, manage rewards, prevent fraud, and, where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements, personalize advertising. Ads may be contextual or personalized, depending on the provider, your privacy choices, and device settings. We communicate your optional-services choice to LevelPlay and stop requesting new third-party ads when optional services are disabled. Providers also apply their own privacy policies and device-level privacy settings.

Promotional content and external links

The App may download promotional images, icons, or videos from our content servers to recommend other apps. Downloads may occur even when optional analytics and third-party advertising are disabled. Content servers and their hosting providers may receive an IP address, request time, requested file, and basic connection information to deliver content and maintain security.

Google Play links may include a promotion-source parameter indicating which App promotion you used. If you open a store link, submit a Google Play review, or visit an external website, Google or the destination service handles that interaction under its own policy. We do not receive your Google Account password.

Video recording, saving, and sharing

The App can create a video of its rendered pond scene using built-in audio. This is processed locally; it does not record your camera, microphone, or other apps. Temporary recordings are stored in the App's cache. We do not automatically upload their contents to our servers.

If you save a video, the App writes it to your device's media library. On Android 8 and 9, it requests storage-write permission for this action; newer versions use Android's media-saving APIs. If you share a recording, the App grants access to that video to the destination you choose through the Android share interface. The receiving app or service may upload or otherwise process it under its own policy.

Device features

The App uses internet access and network status for online services, and vibration for touch feedback. Tilt readings from the accelerometer are processed on your device for pond visual effects and are not sent to us. The live wallpaper runs through Android's wallpaper service and does not require access to your private messages, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Feedback and support

You can voluntarily submit feedback or report a problem through Settings > Feedback / Bug Report. When you select Submit, the App sends the text you enter, any reasons you select, and the following automatically attached information:

  • App version and build number;
  • Android version;
  • Device brand and model;
  • Screen dimensions in pixels.

We use this information to investigate technical problems, understand feedback, improve the App, and handle support requests. The in-app form does not require an account or email address. It does not automatically attach advertising identifiers, Android ID, IMEI, contacts, photos, recordings, or precise location. We do not include your feedback text in our Firebase Analytics events or Crashlytics logs.

In-app submissions are sent over HTTPS to our feedback service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes the request to provide the hosting service and may receive your IP address, request time, and basic connection information for delivery, troubleshooting, and security. Opening or cancelling the feedback dialog does not itself submit a feedback report.

Feedback is initiated by you and is separate from optional analytics, crash reporting, and third-party advertising. You can submit feedback even when those optional services are disabled. You can choose not to submit, or close the dialog before submitting.

If you choose Write an email, the App opens your email app with a draft containing your entered text, selected reasons, and the same basic App and device information. You can review or edit the draft before sending it. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send, including any attachments you choose to add. Our email service provider processes these communications to deliver and store them. Please avoid including sensitive information unrelated to your request.

  • Privacy Choices: open the App's Settings and select Privacy Choices to allow or refuse optional analytics, crash reporting, and third-party advertising together. We do not sell personal information for money; some advertising disclosures may qualify as a sale or sharing under certain U.S. state laws.

Because the App has no account, an email address alone may not identify installation-based records. We may request limited information reasonably necessary to locate relevant records and verify a request. Turning off optional services or deleting the App does not automatically delete information previously received by providers. Contact us for assistance with deletion requests; providers may also offer their own privacy-request tools. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.
