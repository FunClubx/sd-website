3. Videos and Files on Your Device

When you choose your own video using Android’s system file picker, TikWall receives access only to the file you select. The App may copy that video into its private local storage so it can be used as a live wallpaper. TikWall does not upload your selected personal video to Guangzhou Suide Trading Co., Ltd, Firebase, Unity, Cloudflare, or our feedback service.

Downloaded wallpapers, thumbnails, promotional media, preferences, favorites, and temporary files may be stored locally on your device. You can remove temporary downloads using the App’s cache-clearing feature where available, by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App.
